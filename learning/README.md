# Learn the atomtronic isolation project

**Question:** When does a directional atomic circuit distribute quantum entanglement, and why does access to its reflected output change the answer?

The single educational anchor is **Weedbrook et al., _Gaussian Quantum Information_, Reviews of Modern Physics 84, 621–669 (2012)**. The [source-to-project bridge](WEEDBROOK_BRIDGE.md) uses the freely available arXiv version, identifies precise sections, converts conventions, and works through a complete collection-threshold example. It separates the review's foundations from the project's additional proofs and atomic assumptions.

Follow the [30-milestone map](MILESTONES.md) alongside the bridge. M01–M13 build from individual modes to the complete communication interface. M14–M30 connect noise constraints, the all-input theorem, achievable rates, and the physical construction. The map is a common set of understanding targets; it does not depend on a reader's background or assessment history.
